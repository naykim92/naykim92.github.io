RESEARCH

Research Interests

From linguistic structure to human commonsense

01

Computational Linguistics

How can linguistic knowledge contribute to better language models? I explore the intersection of linguistics and computer science to deepen our understanding of human language.

02

Korean NLP

I study representation learning and linguistic competence evaluation informed by the writing system and phonology of Korean, connecting linguistic knowledge with model design and evaluation.

03

Moral Commonsense & AI

How do people develop implicit moral commonsense, and can AI learn it? I explore explicit representations of social norms and computational models of moral cognition.

About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher working on computational linguistics, Korean natural language processing, and human moral commonsense in AI. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University, following a B.A. in Korean Language and Literature at Kyungpook National University. My background in both fields shapes my approach to understanding language and human cognition through AI.
